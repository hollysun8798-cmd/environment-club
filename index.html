🌱 Our Mission

The environmental club is dedicated to ensuring our community is healthy and safe for years to come. We aim to spread awareness and inspire people to volunteer for the sake of our surroundings.

By the end of the year, we hope to have completed beach and park cleanups, implemented sustainable composting at school, improved recycling throughout campus, and fostered an overall appreciation for our environment.
